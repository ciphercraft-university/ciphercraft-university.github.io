HOW IT WORKS

The Scytale cipher was used by ancient Spartans for military communication. It's a transposition cipher that rearranges the order of letters without changing them.

  1. Setup: A strip of parchment is wrapped around a rod with a specific diameter (the key).
  2. Writing: The message is written along the length of the rod (row by row in our grid).
  3. Reading: The parchment is unwrapped, and the message is read across the width (column by column in our grid).
  4. Decryption: The receiver wraps the parchment around an identical rod and reads along the length.

The security depends entirely on the enemy not knowing the rod's diameter (the key). In this visualization, the key represents the number of columns in our grid.
