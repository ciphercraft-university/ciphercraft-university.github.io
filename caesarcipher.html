HOW CAESAR CIPHER WORKS

The Caesar cipher is a simple and widely known encryption technique. It is a type of substitution cipher in which each letter in the plaintext is replaced by a letter some fixed number of positions down the alphabet.



ENCRYPTION PROCESS

1. The original text is converted to uppercase.

2. Each letter is shifted by a fixed number of positions down the alphabet (e.g., a shift of 3 would turn 'A' into 'D', 'B' into 'E', and so on).

3. Non-alphabetic characters (like spaces, numbers, and punctuation) are left unchanged.

4. The new shifted letters form the ciphertext.
